# Wallbreaker Roadmap — Ideas from deepteam/pentestagent deepdive

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Source:** Crosscheck analysis (Agnes+Luna) of `confident-ai/deepteam` + `GH05TCREW/pentestagent`.
Full report: `~/workspace/redteam-repos/DEEPDIVE.md`

> deepteam = direct competitor of wallbreaker's attack engine.
> pentestagent = competitor of the harness layer.
> Neither has a curated jailbreak library (L1B3RT4S) or a fixed benchmark — those are
> wallbreaker's moats. Don't give them up.

---

## 1. Parseltongue per-turn hook

**From:** deepteam `turn_level_attacks`

Apply transform encoding **per-turn inside** PAIR/Crescendo loops, not just on the
initial seed. deepteam shows this raises bypass rates in multi-turn attacks.

**Work:** Add a `turn_transform` hook to the attack-loop context; each refinement
round may re-encode the prompt through a Parseltongue chain before firing.

## 2. Pre-judge reply screening

**From:** deepteam

Heuristic screening **before** calling the LLM judge — skip obvious refusals and
empty responses. Cuts judge-call costs significantly on large runs.

**Work:** `judging.pre_screen(response) -> bool`; wire into `grade()` fast path.

## 3. BadLikertJudge

**From:** deepteam (novel attack)

An attack that targets the **evaluator role itself** (hijack the judge's scoring
frame). Add it as a new attack module — AND harden wallbreaker's own judge
against role hijacking at the same time.

**Work:** New `tools/bad_likert.py`; judge prompt gets anti-hijack guard.

## 4. Declarative playbooks as dataclasses

**From:** pentestagent `Phase(name/objective/techniques)`

Define attacks declaratively (`Phase` dataclass rendered into prompts) instead of
hand-written template strings. Easier to compose, test, and extend.

**Work:** `tools/playbook.py` with `Phase` dataclass; migrate PAIR/TAP/Crescendo
templates.

## 5. Per-case cost tracking

**From:** deepteam

Track token/API-call cost **per test case**. Essential for large runs so the
budget is visible before it explodes.

**Work:** Cost accumulator on the run context; surfaced in `finish()` summary
and the session scorecard.
